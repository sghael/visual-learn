What the dates mean

DeepSeek V3 appears first because it has the earliest release date among models measured on the scoring method used here. It was not necessarily the most intelligent model available then. The early curve is an incomplete view of the frontier.

Artificial Analysis revised its Intelligence Index in September 2026. Scores from before and after that change cannot be compared directly. This chart uses measurements from September 5 onward. The archive has 36 retired models with no measurement on that basis, including GPT-5 and GPT-5.1 from 2025. They are absent from this curve, which is the main reason the early dates are sparse. The archive methodology explains how it separates scoring periods.

There is a separate coverage gap on the live Artificial Analysis site: some models have an Index score but no reported cost per Index task. Its listings for Claude 4 Opus and GPT-5.2 are examples. These models are also outside the archived set used here.
