What the dates mean

DeepSeek V3 is the first dot because it has the earliest release date in the archived data with both an Intelligence Index score and a cost per Index task. It was not necessarily the most intelligent model available then. The early chart is an incomplete view of the frontier.

Some higher-scoring 2025 models have a score but no reported cost per Index task, so they cannot be placed on this cost axis. For example, Artificial Analysis lists scores for Claude 4 Opus and GPT-5.2, while its cost-per-task comparison has no data for those models.

The date slider reveals models by release date. Every dot stays at its September 2026 score and cost. The animation does not reconstruct past scores or prices; its frontier covers only the models that can be plotted from the available data.
