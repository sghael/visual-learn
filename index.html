Visual Learn

How machine-learning systems work, explained with figures you can operate

Each explainer follows one idea from the problem it solves to the mechanism that solves it. The figures run in your browser: move a slider, step through a computation, compare two designs side by side. Where a figure uses a simplified or simulated model, its caption says so.
